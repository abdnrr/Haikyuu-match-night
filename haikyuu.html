SET 1 — ROTATION

Every Player Has a Position to Grow Into

Haikyuu!! follows Karasuno High's volleyball club as they climb back from a losing streak, built around a short middle blocker who refuses to believe height decides anything.

The series is famous for treating every rally like it matters — full sequences given to a single serve, a single read, a single decision made in under a second. It's less about raw talent than about what a team can build together in the half-second before the ball lands.
